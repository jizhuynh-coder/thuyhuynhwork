Little Dentist Campaign

Client
P/S Vietnam
Responsibility
Campaign Idea, Art Direction & Design

Problem
P/S needs to recruit little brand’s ambassadors from kids, age 6 - 12, to join 2016 world oral health celebration day.

Challenge
The process must be simple and interesting for little kids. Moms are the key contacts, as kids at these ages can not register by themselves. Thus, the program needs to speak to both the kids and their moms.

Solution
We create the little dentist academy, where kids can learn/demonstrate their abilities while playing, and moms would love to show and spread how proud they are on their kids.

The cleanliness and simplicity are kept consistently with P/S branding. While the cuteness of P/S mascot and lively color scheme make sure the visual appealing to kids and suggest the optimistic attitude of the whole campaign.
